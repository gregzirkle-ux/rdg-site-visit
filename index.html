Send as a Link

  1. Save the report to your cloud

    In the share menu choose Save to Files, then OneDrive, iCloud Drive or Google Drive. Or pick the OneDrive or Google Drive app directly.

  2. Copy a share link

    Open the file in that cloud app and copy a link that anyone with the link can open.

    OneDrive

    Open the OneDrive app and find the report. Tap the three dots, then Share. Check the link setting says Anyone with the link can view, then tap Copy link.

    iCloud Drive

    Open the Files app and find the report. Press and hold it, tap Share, then tap the line under the title and set Who can access to Anyone with the link. Tap Copy Link.

    Google Drive

    Open the Google Drive app and find the report. Tap the three dots, then Share and Manage access. Set General access to Anyone with the link, then tap Copy link.

  3. Paste the link and send

    The email opens with the link and a short message already written.

Why back up

Your projects, visits and photos live only on this phone, inside this app. Nothing goes to RDG or the cloud on its own. If the app is deleted, Safari or Chrome data is cleared, or you move to a new phone, that work is gone.

A backup is one file that holds everything. Keep it off this phone and you can bring it all back at any time.

Back up

Where to keep it. OneDrive is best, because you can open it from any office computer. In the share menu choose Save to Files, then OneDrive, and make a folder called RDG Site Visit Backups. iCloud Drive or Google Drive work too. Do not leave it only in Downloads on this phone; it would be lost with the phone.
When. After each visit you issue, or at least once a week. Always before you delete or reinstall the app.

Restore

Use this after reinstalling the app or on a new phone. Choose your backup file from OneDrive, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or Files. The Recovery file inside a saved visit package works too. Projects already on this phone are kept, and if the file has the same project you choose which copy to keep.

How to reinstall the app safely
  1. Back up here and save the file to OneDrive.
  2. Delete the Site Visit icon from your home screen.
  3. Open the Site Visit link in Safari (Chrome on Android) and choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Open the new icon, tap Backup and Restore, then Restore from a backup file.

Save Visit Package

Everything for the project folder

One ZIP file with the Word report, every original photo, a photo index and a recovery file. Save it in the project folder so the visit can be archived to the server.

  1. Tap Share or Save ZIP below.
  2. Choose Save to Files, then OneDrive (or your cloud app), and open the project folder. On Android the ZIP goes to Downloads; upload it from your cloud app.
  3. On a computer, extract the ZIP to reveal the organized SVR folder.

Nothing uploads by itself. Check the upload has finished in your cloud app before removing photos from this phone.

Phone Storage

Completed visits can have their photos removed from this phone 30 days after completion. Nothing is removed automatically. Check the report, photos and visit package are in your cloud before removing a local copy.

Visit summaries, numbering and item history stay here. Photos needed by open items stay too. To view removed photos or rebuild an old report, restore the visit package's recovery file.
